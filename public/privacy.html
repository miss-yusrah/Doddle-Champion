Privacy

Last updated: 16 September 2026.

What we store

Scores, settings, and daily missions are stored locally on your device. Challenge links carry a bout in the URL fragment and are not sent to a server by this app.

MiniPay

When opened in MiniPay, the app reads the active wallet account only to create an in-game alias and, if a tracker contract is configured, to save finished matches on Celo. It does not request message signatures and does not display your full address as your name.

Payments

Gameplay is free. Network fees for optional on-chain saves are paid in stablecoin via MiniPay fee abstraction when you confirm a transaction.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project repository or the email used on the MiniPay intake form.
